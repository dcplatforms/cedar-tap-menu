Dry Hire

The rig is yours.
You pour it your way.

Rent Cedar Tap's 4-tap mobile trailer, deliver it to your location, and fill it with whatever you're pouring. Perfect for wineries, breweries, and distilleries that want to extend their brand off-site without staffing constraints.

  • 4-tap cold-service trailer, delivered & set up
  • Compatible with half barrels, corny kegs, and sixtels
  • Off-grid capable — no power hookup required
  • Full setup and breakdown included in rate
  • Operator manual + remote support on call
  • Ideal for private events, venue activations, and B2B rentals
ABC licensing, product sourcing, and staffing are the renter's responsibility under dry hire. Cedar Tap provides the equipment and delivery only.

Full Service Hire

You focus on your event.
We handle every pour.

Cedar Tap manages everything from product sourcing to health permits, ABC licensing, staffing, setup, and breakdown. Ideal for festivals, corporate events, and private venues that want a turnkey premium bar experience with zero overhead.

  • Product sourcing and keg delivery coordinated end-to-end
  • VA ABC banquet licensing handled per event ($85 cost pass-through)
  • Health permit application and compliance managed
  • Trained pouring staff on-site for event duration
  • Point-of-sale via Square — tap-to-pay and QR ordering
  • Post-event revenue report and pour analytics provided
Minimum 6-hour booking. Available for events up to 5,000 guests. Multi-day and multi-city bookings available. Spirits events require 12-day ABC lead time.

How it works

1

Reach out with your event details

Send us the date, location, expected attendance, and service tier you're interested in. We'll confirm availability within 24 hours.

2

We build your pour menu

Based on your audience and event type, we recommend the optimal product mix — coffee, craft beer, wine, kombucha, or spirits — and send a custom quote.

3

Permits, sourcing, and logistics handled

For Full Service bookings, Cedar Tap coordinates ABC banquet licensing, health permits, keg delivery, and staffing. Dry Hire clients receive equipment on the morning of the event.

4

Show up, pour, and get paid

We arrive early, set up, and run the tap room. Payments are processed via Square tap-to-pay and QR ordering. Post-event you receive a full pour report and settlement.
