Calculation Methodology — Full-Keg Model (Updated 2026-06-08)

Model change: Previous model used 5.1L fill draws from larger kegs. This version treats 1 full keg as the cost and volume unit per tap per event.


Keg volumes: Wine & cold brew: 5 gal = 18,927 mL · Beer sixtels: 5.16 gal = 19,531 mL

Pours per keg = floor(keg_volume_mL / mL_per_pour)

Gross revenue = pours × sell_price

Net profit = gross_revenue − keg_cost

Margin = net_profit / gross_revenue

Break-even pours = ceil(keg_cost / sell_price)


Assumes full sell-out unless utilization scenario specified. Excludes: ABC banquet license ($85/event), CO₂/N₂ gas, labor, dry hire fee, transport, cups (~$0.07–0.15/cup). Keg prices from dckegs.com as of 2026-06-08. ~5–10% waste factor not applied to pours (conservative — actual yield slightly lower). Revenue model assumes all pours of each keg are sold; partial keg at end of event reduces gross proportionally.
