Revenue Models

Four Ways to Generate Revenue
on the Cedar Tap Platform

Each model is structured around a different relationship type — whether you supply the product, own the venue, activate at events, or want complete end-to-pour service.

Tap Placement

Featured Brand on Tap

Your product poured directly through Cedar Tap's dispensing platform at events, markets, and brand activations across the DC Metro. We source, transport, chill, and serve your product — you supply the kegs and we deliver your brand to a new audience.

  • Your brand on 1 of 4 taps at every event we work
  • Co-branded QR menus + your logo on-site signage
  • Per-pour sales data delivered post-event
  • Sampling and tasting flight options available
  • Ideal for breweries, roasters, RTD brands, wineries
Revenue Structure
Revenue Share or Flat Product Supply
Co-Brand Activation

Branded Deployment Partner

Cedar Tap deploys at your event or venue as a co-branded extension of your brand — your logo, your product, your story poured through a premium mobile tap room. Ideal for brand launches, member appreciation events, and tasting activations.

  • Full trailer branded to your identity for the event
  • Custom co-branded menus and digital assets
  • Your social content shot on-site during event
  • Staff trained on your product story and talking points
  • Ideal for fitness centers, distilleries, event venues
Revenue Structure
Flat Event Fee + Optional Pour Revenue Share
Venue Partnership

Recurring On-Site Activation

Cedar Tap becomes a recurring revenue-generating fixture at your venue — weekly tastings, monthly retail off-site events, or seasonal activations. We handle ABC licensing, permits, product sourcing, staffing, and the pour. You provide the location and promotion.

  • Weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly deployment schedule
  • Virginia ABC banquet licensing handled per event (~$85)
  • Off-grid — no venue power or plumbing required
  • Shared revenue per pour or flat venue hosting fee
  • Ideal for wineries, distilleries, sporting complexes
Revenue Structure
Revenue Share or Monthly Flat Hosting Rate
Platform Access

Equipment Rental (Dry Hire)

Access the full Cedar Tap dispensing platform — trailer, 4-tap array, and embedded cashless checkout — with your own product. You run the bar program, we provide the infrastructure. Ideal for brands and operators with existing supply chains who need mobile distribution hardware.

  • Full Cedar Tap unit with QR scan-to-pay + Square POS
  • Bring-your-own-beverage (BYOB kegs)
  • Hourly · half-day · full-day · weekend rates
  • Delivery, setup, and teardown included
  • Ideal for corporate events, private activations
Revenue Structure
Flat Rental: $150/hr · $500/half · $1,000/day · $1,750/wknd

CedarTap Cashless Ecosystem

The Infrastructure Behind Every Pour

Every deployment comes with our proprietary payment and analytics stack — no additional hardware or software required from the brand or venue.

⚙️

4-Tap Dispensing Array

Multi-product capable — nitro cold brew, draft latte, beer, wine, kombucha, or any kegged beverage. Chilled, pressurized, and pour-ready on deployment.

📱

QR Scan-to-Pay

Each tap has a unique QR code. Customers scan, select size, and pay in under 10 seconds — no app download, no account creation, no friction.

💳

Tap-to-Pay + Square POS

Apple Pay, Google Pay, contactless cards. Square terminal for higher-volume service. Zero cash handling — every transaction digital and timestamped.

📊

Real-Time Revenue Dashboard

Live transaction data accessible during event. Post-event report delivered within 24 hours — total pours, revenue by product, peak period, average transaction.

🔋

Fully Off-Grid

Onboard power system — no venue electrical hookup required. Operates in parks, lots, fields, rooftops, and any outdoor space with a clear path for the trailer.

🛡️

Insured & Permit-Ready

Commercial general liability coverage. Virginia ABC banquet license capability. Food handler certification. Fully compliant for county event venues and private property.
