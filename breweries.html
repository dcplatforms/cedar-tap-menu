Zero Overhead

No staff to schedule. No equipment to haul. No permits to chase. We arrive set up and ready — your beer on our taps in under 30 minutes.

Brand First

Fully Insured

Cedar Tap carries its own liability insurance and handles ABC licensing documentation for every event. Your team stays focused on brewing, not compliance.
